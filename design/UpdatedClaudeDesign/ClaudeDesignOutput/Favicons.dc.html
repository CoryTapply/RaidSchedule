Favicon variants
Ten directions — six with a Z, four calendar-only. Each shown at 96px, then at real favicon sizes (32px, 16px) on a dark and a light tab ground.
01 Filled square, Z cut out
Solid accent tile, Z knocked out to the page ground. Heaviest silhouette — reads cleanly at 16px.
02 Outlined calendar, accent Z
Light 2px frame with the Z in accent. Closest to the app chrome; the thin frame softens at 16px.
03 Z as the header band
The Z lives in the header strip instead of the grid, leaving the body free for day cells.
04 Z from the grid cells
The Z is drawn by lit day cells — calendar and letter are one object. Strong at 32px, loses the letter at 16px.
05 Z badge on a calendar
Neutral calendar with an accent Z badge in the corner. The badge stays legible when the frame does not.
06 Z monogram, calendar rings only
Reduced to two binder rings plus a large Z. The most legible small mark here; least obviously a calendar.
07 Calendar only — three-week grid
No letter. Three rows of cells echo the actual three-week view, the middle row lit as the active lockout.
08 Calendar only — single lit date
One accent day on a quiet frame: raid night is this day. Simplest silhouette, holds at 16px.
09 Calendar only — hatched day
Carries the signup hatch from the app into the mark: one hatched cell, one solid. Detail is lost below 32px.
10 Calendar only — filled tile
Inverted: accent tile with the calendar knocked out. Strongest presence in a crowded tab bar.
