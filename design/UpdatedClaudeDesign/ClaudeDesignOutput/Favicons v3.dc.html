Favicon variants — sparse family (from 15)
Ten takes on events-only: no frame, no header, the three-week grid implied by where the marks sit. 96px, then 32px and 16px on a dark and a light tab ground.
21 Three events, nothing else
15 stripped bare — no dim cells at all. Three marks in grid positions; the calendar is implied by placement only.
22 Faint grid, three events
Dim cells lifted from #232538 to #2b2e45 so the grid is just perceptible on a dark tab, invisible on light.
23 Dots, not cells
Same sparse layout as circles. Softer; loses the day-cell association the squares carry.
24 Larger events
Same three positions, cells grown to 4.6. Best 16px legibility of this family.
25 Two states
One outlined (signed up), two solid (confirmed) — the app’s core distinction inside the mark.
26 Class colors
Three events in three class colors instead of the accent. Carries the app’s whole idea; breaks the one-accent rule.
27 Six events, two per week
Denser schedule — a busier raid week. More texture, less punch at 16px.
28 Pills
Events as wide pills spanning two columns. Strong horizontal rhythm, reads as blocked-out nights.
29 One primary, two secondary
Next raid larger and full accent, the other two smaller and dimmed — hierarchy inside three marks.
30 Inverted tile
Accent ground with the three events knocked out to the page dark. Loudest of the sparse family.
