Favicon variants — round two
Three-week grid, no header or frame outline, events as dots and lit cells. Each shown at 96px, then at 32px and 16px on a dark and a light tab ground.
11 Dot matrix, three events
7×3 dots, three lit. Reads as a month of days without any frame; the smallest dots vanish at 16px.
12 Cell grid, three events
5×3 rounded cells, three lit accent. Cleanest of the set at 16px — the lit cells hold as shapes.
13 Lockout row lit
One full week lit instead of scattered events. The strongest horizontal read; loses the per-event idea.
14 Row bars with event dots
Three week bars, accent dots sitting on them. Very legible small; the dots merge into the bars at 16px.
15 Sparse — events only
Dim cells drop away entirely; only the three raid nights are drawn. Most distinctive, least calendar-like.
16 Two-tone events
Solid cells for confirmed, outlined for signed-up — the app’s two states carried into the mark.
17 Diagonal run
Lit cells step down and across — implies a recurring schedule. Pleasing shape, weakest at 16px.
18 Light grid on accent tile
Inverted: accent ground, dark cells, light events. Loudest in a tab bar; the grid flattens when small.
19 Wide cells, one big event
Chunkier 4×3 cells with a single double-width event block. The most robust silhouette here.
20 Dot matrix, lit column
Same night every week — one column lit down all three rows. Clear pattern, holds better than scattered dots.
