Opens every section. Extracted from markup the source repeated on each one.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="This tier"
  title="Attendance"
  description="Counts every character signed to a Tuesday or Friday lockout."
  action={<Button size="sm">Export</Button>}
/>
```

The eyebrow is the system's signature micro-label: 10.5px mono, uppercase, .14em tracking, 45% opacity. Sentence case the title even though the eyebrow is uppercase.
